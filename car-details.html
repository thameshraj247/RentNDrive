Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting with RentNDrive.

What documents do I need to rent this vehicle?
You will need a valid driver's license held for at least 1 year (or international driving permit if visiting from abroad), an official passport or photo ID, and a credit or debit card in the primary renter's name.
How does the digital key mobile unlock work?
Once your reservation is confirmed and verified, your digital key is activated in your customer dashboard. Simply walk up to the car, press "Unlock" in your phone browser, and the doors will unlock immediately. The push-to-start button is ready.
Is there a mileage limit on this car?
All standard rentals include 200 complimentary miles per day (cumulative for multi-day rentals). For extended cross-country trips, unlimited mileage packages are available at checkout.
When is the security deposit refunded?
The security deposit is simply a pre-authorization hold on your card. It is released automatically within 24 to 48 hours following vehicle return and inspection.
